Torque conversion

Torque wrench + extension

TW = TA × L ÷ (L + E)
L = center of the handle grip to the center of the drive. E = center of the drive to the center of the fastener, measured along the wrench's line. Use a negative E if the adapter points back toward the handle. An adapter at 90° to the wrench needs no correction.

Sheet metal layout

BA = (0.01743·R + 0.0078·T) × degrees
K = tan(degrees ÷ 2)   SB = K × (R + T)
Flat length (one bend) = A + B − 2·SB + BA. Make the sight line one radius in from the bend line, on the side that goes under the brake nose.
